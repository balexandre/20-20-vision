    What the numbers mean

    🔭

    Nearsighted (minus)

    The eye focuses too strongly, so far things blur while your phone stays sharp. A −2.00 eye sees clearly only up to 50 cm.

    🔬

    Farsighted (plus)

    The eye focuses too weakly. Young eyes can squeeze harder to compensate, which is tiring. With age, that trick stops working.

    🌀

    Astigmatism

    The cornea is shaped more like a rugby ball than a football, so lines in one direction blur more than others. Very common, often mild.

    🦒

    Presbyopia

    Everyone's focusing lens stiffens with age. Around 45 your arms start getting "too short" to read, even with perfect distance vision.
